Is it due?

Signs your air con needs a regas

Every car air con system loses a little gas over time. It happens slowly, so it's easy to miss until summer.

Blowing warm, or just cool

The air comes out, it just never gets properly cold, even on full. That's the most common sign it's low on gas.

Takes ages to cool down

If you're well into the drive before the cabin feels comfortable, the system is working harder than it should.

Not done in years

If you can't remember the last regas, or you haven't used the air con since last summer, it's worth a check before the heat arrives.

Went warm suddenly, or only a few months after a regas? That usually means a leak, and new gas would just leak out again. We'll find the cause first. See air con repairs.

How it works

What happens at your regas

  1. Check it over

    We run the air con, check the temperature at the vents and look over the compressor, hoses and condenser.

  2. Test for leaks

    We take the old gas out and check the system holds a vacuum. If it doesn't, there's a leak, and we'll tell you first.

  3. Regas

    We refill it with the right amount of gas for your car, as set by the manufacturer.

  4. New filter, final check

    We fit your free cabin filter and check it's blowing cold before you pick it up.

If we find anything else, we call you first. Nothing extra gets done unless you say yes.

Questions about air con regas

How do I know if it needs a regas or a repair?

If it's slowly got less cold over a year or two, a regas is usually the fix. If it went warm suddenly, makes a noise or smells musty, it's more likely a fault. Either way, we check the system first and tell you which it is.

How often does car air con need a regas?

There's no set schedule. Every system loses a little gas over time, so when it stops getting as cold as it used to, it's time for a check.

Is the cabin filter really free?

Yes. Book an air con regas this summer and the new cabin filter is on us.

What if a regas doesn't fix it?

Then something has failed or is leaking. We'll tell you what we found and what it'll take to fix. Nothing gets done until you say yes.

Does it matter what car I drive?

Tell us the make, model and year when you book. Some newer cars use a different type of air con gas, so we'll confirm what yours needs before you come in.

Can you do it with my service or roadworthy?

Yes. Mention it when you book and we'll look at the air con while the car's in.
